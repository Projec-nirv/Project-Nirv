Project NIRV (Non-Linear Intelligence Research Vector) is a **research-driven initiative** dedicated to advancing understanding of **systems intelligence**, **infrastructure evolution**, **strategic forecasting**, and **non-linear systems dynamics**. We build products, publish research and insights, and explore emerging systems across technology, markets, society, and infrastructure.

We conduct research across interconnected domains: understanding how complex systems operate through feedback loops and emergent behavior; analyzing how infrastructure constrains growth and economic capacity; forecasting structural inflection points in markets and economies; and identifying macroeconomic vulnerabilities before they become crises.

Our work examines systems, markets, and infrastructure without assuming linear scaling. Research is grounded in verified data sources, explicit structural reasoning, and non-linear analysis frameworks. Our commitment is clarity: understand how systems operate, why they fail, where constraints bind, and how they will evolve.
