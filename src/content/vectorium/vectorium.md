## Vectorium: Applied Research Ecosystem

Vectorium is where Project NIRV research becomes experimental systems and operational infrastructure. Research without application remains theoretical. Application without research becomes engineering noise. Vectorium bridges both.

### What We Build

**Experimental Systems** – Operational tools designed to solve specific intelligence gaps, attribution problems, or infrastructure analysis challenges. Built with research-grade rigor and measurable outcomes.

**Intelligence Platforms** – Systems that bridge raw data and actionable insight through systematic analysis and cross-platform verification.

### How We Work

Each system follows a consistent approach:

1. **Research Foundation** – Grounded in Project NIRV research on systems dynamics and platform behavior
2. **Operational Focus** – Designed to solve real, measurable problems
3. **Verification First** – Built with signal integrity and verification as primary concerns
4. **Structured Documentation** – Systems are documented as research infrastructure, not products
5. **Feedback Loop** – Operational findings generate new research questions
