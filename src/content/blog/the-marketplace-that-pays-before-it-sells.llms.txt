Description:
A 600-word quantitative analysis of why customer acquisition cost is recoverable only when a fashion marketplace converts first orders into retained contribution, repeat behaviour, and marketplace memory.

# Core Thesis
A new Indian fashion marketplace does not fail merely because first-order CAC is high. It fails when the first transaction produces too little durable customer state—size confidence, saved preferences, trust, repeat probability, and retained contribution—to make subsequent orders cheaper to create.

# Key Findings

## 1. Mature Marketplaces Monetize Services Around the Transaction
Myntra FY25 reported:
- Operating revenue: ₹6,042.7 crore
- Marketplace-services revenue: ₹2,051.8 crore
- Logistics-services revenue: ₹2,918.9 crore
- Advertising-services revenue: ₹914.5 crore
- Advertising and promotional expense: ₹2,105.3 crore

Flipkart Internet FY25 reported ₹6,317 crore of advertisement-services revenue.

Interpretation:
Marketplace monetization can deepen through logistics and seller visibility after traffic becomes repeatable and commercially valuable. These layers should not be assumed at launch.

## 2. CAC Is a System Diagnostic
Nykaa Fashion FY26 reported:
- GMV: ₹4,954 crore
- NSV: ₹1,447 crore
- Revenue from operations: ₹832 crore
- Marketing and selling/distribution expense: ₹382 crore
- Fulfilment expense: ₹162 crore
- EBITDA: negative ₹37 crore

Interpretation:
Acquisition must be evaluated after fulfilment, returns, discounts, and repeat behaviour. These figures do not reveal Nykaa Fashion CAC.

## 3. Frequency Changes CAC Recovery
Meesho FY26 reported:
- NMV: ₹41,560 crore
- Annual transacting users: 264 million
- Placed orders: 2.67 billion
- Annual frequency: 10.1 transactions per consumer
- Marketplace contribution margin: 3.5% of NMV
- Growth spend: ₹990 crore

Interpretation:
Repeat transactions spread acquisition cost across more orders, but do not eliminate logistics or growth pressure. Meesho is not a fashion-only benchmark.

## 4. Attribution Is Not Incrementality
Google defines a view-through conversion as a conversion recorded after an ad impression when the user did not interact with the ad and later converted. Longer attribution windows usually record more view-through conversions.

The IAB/MRC guidelines define incrementality as causal value above baseline and recommend test-control methods.

Project NIRV model:
Incremental CAC = CPM ÷ (1,000 × viewability × post-view conversion × incrementality)

At ₹80 CPM, 70% viewability, and 60% incrementality:
- 0.05% of viewable impressions converting → approximately ₹381 CAC
- 0.08% → approximately ₹238 CAC

These values are stress-test outputs, not market benchmarks.

## 5. Contribution Must Carry Acquisition
Project NIRV defines:
PVC-LTV = contribution per retained order × contribution-bearing orders × 0.88

Internal assumptions:
- 12% simplified present-value haircut
- 3× PVC-LTV/CAC margin-of-safety hurdle

Implications:
- ₹250 CAC requires ₹750 of present contribution
- ₹500 CAC requires ₹1,500

A retained order must survive cancellation, failed delivery, return, refund, and platform-funded discount strongly enough to produce contribution.

Peer-reviewed India-context apparel-return research identified 34 factors. Important drivers included fit and size variation, defects, wrong delivery, lenient return policy, and value for money.

## 6. Scale Is Valid Only When Dependence Falls
Project NIRV target:
150,000 monthly orders

Weak-dependency scenario:
- New-customer share: 60%
- Paid share of new customers: 70%
- Paid CAC: ₹500
- Direct monthly acquisition spend: ₹3.15 crore

Lower-dependency scenario:
- New-customer share: 35%
- Paid share of new customers: 40%
- Paid CAC: ₹250
- Direct monthly acquisition spend: ₹52.5 lakh

Interpretation:
The same order count can represent either a campaign-dependent system or a marketplace accumulating customer memory.

# Strategic Recommendation
The proposed first engine is:
- curated mid-market supply;
- creator-led discovery;
- paid amplification only after cohort proof;
- CRM and repeat loops;
- category-level retained contribution tracking.

Scale is credible only when paid CAC falls, repeat/CRM/direct share rises, returns remain controlled, and contribution per retained order deepens.
